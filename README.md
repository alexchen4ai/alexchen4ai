### About Me

I am an AI researcher and engineering leader focused on building production-grade generative AI inference systems across data centers and edge devices.

- **At Qualcomm:** I lead all generative AI inference engineering, a **70–80-person organization** spanning **GenieX** and **Qualcomm AI Hub**, serving **1,000+ enterprise customers** across data centers, robotics, PC, mobile, and IoT.
- **Previously:** I founded and led **Nexa AI**, raised **$15M**, built its core inference and model technology, and led the company through its acquisition by Qualcomm.
- **Education:** PhD in Applied Mathematics from Stanford University.
- **Research:** Author of 10+ publications in AI inference, efficient language models, multimodal AI, and model–hardware co-design.

---

### What I Work On

- **Data-center inference:** vLLM, SGLang, and disaggregated inference across NVIDIA GPUs and Qualcomm AI inference accelerator cards.
- **Heterogeneous serving:** Prefill/decode placement, cross-device scheduling, communication, and end-to-end performance optimization.
- **NPU optimization:** Framework-free, pure-C megakernel execution on Qualcomm Hexagon HTP.
- **Cross-chip inference:** Unified deployment across Qualcomm, NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, and Apple processors.
- **Model optimization:** Hardware-aware quantization, operator fusion, memory scheduling, distillation, and model–hardware co-design.

### Featured Work

- **[NexaML / NexaSDK](https://github.com/NexaAI/NexaSDK):** Cross-chip AI inference infrastructure with **8,300+ GitHub stars**. NexaSDK became the technical predecessor to Qualcomm GenieX.
- **Octopus V1–V4:** On-device language models that outperform GPT-4o on function-calling benchmarks.
- **AutoNeural-VL:** Hardware–software co-designed vision-language model optimized for Qualcomm NPU inference.
- **Hyperlink:** Fully local agentic RAG across 10,000+ documents, running state-of-the-art models on Qualcomm NPU.
